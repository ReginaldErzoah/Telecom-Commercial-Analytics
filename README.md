# Telecom Customer Analytics & Commercial Performance

## Project Overview

**Telecom Customer Analytics & Commercial Performance** is a business intelligence project designed to demonstrate how customer, commercial, and financial data can be transformed into actionable insights for telecom business planning and performance management.

The project combines **Excel, Power BI, and Figma** to create an interactive executive reporting solution covering key areas of telecom customer and commercial performance.

The Power BI report was designed with a focus on **management decision-making, KPI monitoring, trend analysis, financial performance, and user experience**.

---

## Business Objective

The objective of the project is to provide decision-makers with a consolidated view of telecom business performance and enable them to:

* Monitor key commercial and financial KPIs
* Evaluate revenue and financial performance
* Analyse customer and business trends
* Identify performance changes and potential areas of concern
* Compare actual performance against relevant targets or benchmarks
* Support data-driven commercial planning and decision-making
* Explore performance interactively through filters and report navigation

## Power BI Report

The final Power BI solution consists of **3 interactive report pages**, supported by:

* Page navigation buttons
* Filter/navigation controls
* Interactive KPI cards
* Financial performance visualizations
* Trend and comparative analysis
* Executive-level reporting layout
* Custom-designed report backgrounds

### Report Pages

#### 1. Executive Summary

Provides a high-level overview of overall telecom business performance, designed for management and executive decision-making.

The page consolidates the most important KPIs and performance indicators into a single view while providing interactive navigation to deeper analysis.

#### 2. Financial Performance

Focuses on the financial and commercial performance of the business, enabling users to monitor revenue-related KPIs, performance trends and financial indicators.

#### 3. Customer / Commercial Analysis

Provides deeper analytical views of customer and commercial performance, allowing users to investigate underlying trends and performance drivers.

## Dashboard Design

The report interface was designed in **Figma** before implementation in Power BI.

The design approach focused on:

* Clean executive presentation
* Consistent visual hierarchy
* Effective use of dashboard space
* Clear KPI communication
* Simple navigation
* Improved user experience
* Consistent corporate-style visual identity

Interactive navigation and filter buttons were incorporated to make the report feel more like a **business application rather than a collection of static charts**.

---

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a typical business intelligence workflow:

```text
Raw Data > Excel Data Preparation > Data Transformation & Validation > Power BI Data Model > DAX Measures & KPIs > Interactive Visualizations > Executive Reporting > Commercial Decision Support
```

The analysis focuses on connecting **customer and commercial performance to financial outcomes**, rather than simply presenting descriptive statistics.

## Project Outcome

The project resulted in a **3-page interactive Power BI business intelligence report** that brings together customer, commercial and financial perspectives in a management-friendly interface.

It demonstrates the ability to move from **raw business data → analytical model → KPI calculations → interactive dashboard → decision-support reporting**, while combining technical analytics with business-oriented visualization and user experience design.
